
Just to quickly explain how these results are laid out.

1.0-1.3: The first four images just show the output from replacing outliers of
    the neural network with machine learning. More so a visual bug-testing
    thing to see if something is going wrong in that process.

In between these plots is when the bending coefficient of the monofilament is
also calculated. After the outlier imputing, but before the homography matrix
change.

1.4: Shows the result of applying a homography matrix to the monofil point
    locations. After deciding the destination points of the 4 corners of the
    square to be at:
        [[0, 200],
        [200, 200],
        [200, 0],
        [0, 0]]
    So in the case of both these datasets; 200 pixels = 2 cm

2.0-2.1: Just show the IFF and Neuron Spikes plotted together at the original
    sampling rate and then at the target sampling rate of the video, in this
    case 30 fps. So both have a downsample from 120000:30

3.0: Just to illustrate the attempted merging of the data from deeplabcut
    and the neuron spikes to get them on the same timeframes. Again, just to
    see if anything looks wrong before continuing.

For all the plots after this, the data is thresholded to keep the ones that
are significant. So rows with the bending coefficient at a certain minimum are
kept, as well as if the row has a neuron spike. All the other rows are not
plotted.

3.1-3.2: Simple RF mapping of the now merged df. With colours being binary for
    the neuron spikes, and size relative to the bending coefficient.

3.3-3.6: Plots to show 3 conditions either individually or altogether;
    KDE density, scatter, and a background frame from the video.

3.7: Then finally, there's the scroll plot over the video. This is primarily to
    show the neuron spikes and/or IFF over the video on its specific frame. A
    more direct visualization of the merging and if a neuron is on when there
    is contact with the skin.

The last two files are the output datasets, in both csv and excel formatting.
In the cases of datasets 3740 and 3741, both have the thresholded data saved,
and 3741 also has the full data saved.
